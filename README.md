# A Jar of Stars — love-star-jar

Little wishes for Mena and Ammar. Ten starters wait in the jar; drop in your own
whenever one comes to you, then **draw a star** to read a wish you left.

Live: https://menaammarpro.github.io/love-star-jar/

## How it works

- **Add a wish** — becomes a new star in the jar (dated).
- **Draw a star** — pulls a random wish out and shows it; it avoids repeating the
  same star twice in a row.
- **Send the jar** — copies a `?star=1` link; whoever opens it sees a note from
  Ammar and draws a star automatically.

## Storage key

- `mena-starjar` — `{ added: [{ id, text, at }] }`
